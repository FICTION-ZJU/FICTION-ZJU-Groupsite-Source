Prof. Dr. {{% mention "Mingshuai Chen" %}} has been awarded the CCF Distinguished Young Scientist Award (Software Field), known formerly as the NASAC Youth Software Innovation Award, by the software-related technical committees of the China Computer Federation (CCF), in recognition of his contributions to *formal verification of programs and hybrid systems*. The award was presented at the closing ceremony of [CCF ChinaSoft 2026](https://chinasoft.ccf.org.cn/2026/) in Tianjin, China.
