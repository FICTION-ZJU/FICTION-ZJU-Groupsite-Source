Our paper titled "[Formal Specification of Linux eBPF Instruction Set Architecture in Sail](/publication/tang-ebpf2026/)" by {{% mention "Yazhou Tang" %}}, Shenghao Yuan, Jean-Pierre Talpin (INRIA), and {{% mention "Mingshuai Chen" %}} has been accepted for presentation at 4th Workshop on eBPF and Kernel Extensions, in Prague, Czech Republic. This short paper presents a Sail formalization of the Linux eBPF ISA that covers all sequential instructions and serves as a single source for generating both a human-readable specification and executable Rocq semantics.
